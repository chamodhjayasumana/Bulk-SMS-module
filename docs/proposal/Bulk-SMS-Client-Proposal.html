Send one clear SMS to many customers, from your own office.

Bulk SMS Platform is a private messaging system for Sri Lankan businesses. Your staff upload a contact list on the office computer, check the numbers, write the message, and send it through an Android phone on the same Wi-Fi. You see what went through, and you can retry the numbers that did not.

2. The Android gateway

The website prepares the campaign. The phone sends it. Keep one Android phone on the office Wi-Fi, with the gateway app open and a SIM that has SMS credit. The phone talks only to the office server, then the mobile network delivers each text.

After Send, the same dashboard shows the campaign moving. Operators do not have to guess whether the list is finished. They can see progress, successful and failed counts, and the numbers still waiting.

How operators follow a live send After Send, the bar moves and the counts update: successful, failed, and still pending. Week 1: install and send a test. Week 1–2: a pilot of 5 to 20 people. Week 2: train the operators. After that, send within the SIM package, not as an unlimited blast.

What you provide

A Windows PC, stable office Wi-Fi, an Android phone for the gateway, an active SIM with SMS credit, and a person who approves every campaign. Message content and customer consent stay your responsibility.

Please read this before a large campaign A normal prepaid or postpaid SIM is not unlimited. The phone must stay on and connected. If the phone gets a new Wi-Fi address, the office setting may need a quick update. The system does not bypass the mobile network or local advertising rules. Questions clients usually ask

Open any question. The short answer is written for a business owner, not only for IT.

Do we have to put our customer list on a public website?

No. The usual setup stays on your office network. Staff open it from the office computer. The Android phone also stays on that local Wi-Fi.

Who is allowed to send?

Only people with a sign-in. Sending is also blocked when the phone gateway is offline, so a campaign cannot leave while the phone is disconnected.

Can the writing assistant send messages on its own?

No. It can suggest wording. A person reviews the text, checks the recipient count, and presses Send.

What if some numbers fail?

They stay visible in the results. Export Failed Numbers gives you a file of just those contacts, ready for a correction or a second try.

What happens next if we want this?

A short call about your volume and languages, a live demo on a local setup, a small pilot, then installation and operator training.
